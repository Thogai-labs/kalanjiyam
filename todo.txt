IMPLEMENTATION PLAN: STRATEGY B (UNIFIED PAGEDOCUMENT MODEL FOR STORAGE)
===============================================================================

Goal:
  Eliminate redundant /ocr/{page_slug}.json.gz storage payloads by deriving 
  bounding boxes directly from the unified PageDocument revision payload 
  (stored in /revisions/{page_slug}/{tag}_v{version}.json.gz).

Benefits at 20 Million Pages Scale:
  - 50% Reduction in S3 Object Count (20M fewer S3 objects)
  - ~200 GB Storage Savings (eliminates duplicate coordinate payloads)
  - 50% Reduction in S3 Write IOPS during OCR processing
  - Single Source of Truth for document layout and coordinates
